# Genome Quality Control Workflow

Genome quality assessment is an important step after assembly to evaluate
assembly continuity, completeness, and potential contamination.

## Recommended workflow

1. Assembly statistics with QUAST
2. Completeness assessment with BUSCO
3. Completeness and contamination assessment with CheckM2
4. Review genome size, GC content, contig count, and N50
5. Compare quality metrics before downstream analysis

## Example tools
- QUAST
- BUSCO
- CheckM2
- NanoPlot
